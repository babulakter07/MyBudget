# MyBudget — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-18_

MyBudget is an offline personal finance app. **It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.**

## What the app stores
Everything you enter (profiles, accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, debts, tags, receipt photos) is stored
only in the app's private storage on your device.

## What the app does not do
- No user accounts, sign-in or cloud sync
- No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs
- No network requests with your data

## Permissions
| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Biometric / fingerprint | Optional app lock |
| Notifications | Optional bill, debt and budget reminders (local only) |
| Camera / photo access (via the system picker) | Optional receipt photos you choose to attach |

## Backups and sharing
Backups and report exports are files created on your device. They leave the device only when **you** choose to
share them (for example to email or a cloud drive). You can protect a backup with a password; if you forget it,
it cannot be recovered.

## Error log
The app keeps a small error log on the device to help diagnose problems. It is never sent automatically; you may
choose to attach it to a support email.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device.
